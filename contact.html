FAQ

Good questions.

What kinds of projects do you finance?

We advise on debt and grant funding for projects of every size, from USD 5,000 SME grants to multi-million dollar debt facilities, across construction, agriculture, energy, mining, transport and creative businesses.

How does the first consultation work?

We start with a conversation about your project, goals and timeline. From there we carry out a financial needs assessment and recommend the right delivery and financing approach.

Do you work with small and youth-led businesses?

Yes. Supporting youth-led businesses is core to our mission. We help with formalisation, grant applications and compliance so smaller businesses can access funding.

Where are you based?

Our office is at Brickwood Apartments, Lantana Road, Westlands, Nairobi (P.O. Box 49-01001). We work with clients across multiple regions.
